The Beginning

It all began in 2023, with a simple match on Bumble.

Their first conversation started with a playful question from him.

“Chan kepanjangannya apa? Chantik?”

She simply replied, “Begitulah.”

The conversation continued, eventually leading to a conversation about their height. He told her he was 178 cm without shoes, while she admitted that she was only 150 cm and felt a little nervous standing beside someone so tall.

“Orang tinggi nggak gigit,” he teased.

“Nggak gigit tapi ngetekin,” she replied.

“Orang yang lebih pendek lebih mudah di-pat pat.”

“Pat pat please.”

He answered with a little pat-pat GIF.

The next day, she asked, “Jadi kapan kamu mau pat pat aku?”

“Virtual apa real life?” he asked.

“Real life lah, virtual nggak berasa.”

And that was all it took. They decided to meet the very next day.

When he arrived to pick her up, however, she was still asleep—leaving him waiting longer than expected. And once they finally got on the road, things didn’t exactly go according to plan. They ended up getting lost because he had entered the wrong destination.

His excuse? He was still new to Jakarta. He was originally from Bandung.

It wasn’t the smoothest first date, but somehow, it didn’t stop them from meeting again. And again.

Before long, those two strangers from Bumble had become two people who simply kept finding reasons to see each other. And somewhere along the way, without either of them realizing exactly when it happened, their story had already begun.
